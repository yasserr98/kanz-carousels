## A — airline costs

[IATA: Unveiling the biggest airline costs](https://www.iata.org/en/publications/newsletters/iata-knowledge-hub/unveiling-the-biggest-airline-costs), published 4 June 2024, reporting WATS **2022** global data: fuel/oil 28.7%; depreciation/amortization 9.1%; flight crew salaries/expenses 8.6%. The date appears on the graphic. Remaining 53.6% is our arithmetic: 100 − 28.7 − 9.1 − 8.6. Regional cost mix differs. These are airline cost shares, not a breakdown of a customer's airfare and not 2026 data.

## B — landed cost

[International Trade Administration: Determine Total Export Price](https://www.trade.gov/determine-total-export-price) lists product price, insurance, freight, tariffs/taxes and other fees as landed-cost components. [Import Tariffs & Fees Overview](https://www.trade.gov/import-tariffs-fees-overview) supports varying tariffs and additional customs/shipping fees. Four graphic groups are editorial categorization; 01–04 are ordinal labels. No specific tariff rate, local legal rule, or measured cost share is asserted.

## C — purchasing power

[Investor.gov: Beginners’ Guide to Asset Allocation, Diversification, and Rebalancing](https://www.investor.gov/additional-resources/general-resources/publications-research/info-sheets/beginners-guide-asset) explains inflation eroding purchasing power. Graphic scenario is explicitly hypothetical: one basket costs 100, then 110 after one year at 10% inflation. Unchanged 100 units buys 100/110 = 90.909…% of the original basket. Bar width 90.909%; displayed result 90.9%. The decline in purchasing power is approximately 9.1%, not 10%; the image does not claim an actual national inflation rate.

## D — buying and renting

[CFPB: Financial considerations of buying a home](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-are-some-of-the-financial-considerations-of-buying-a-home-en-119/) supports upfront/transaction costs, ongoing ownership obligations, maintenance and the implications of moving. [CFPB: Making the decision to rent or buy](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/archive/blog/making-decision-rent-or-buy/) supports comparing total costs and flexibility. [CFPB buying-or-renting tool](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_ymyg-servicemembers-tool_buying-or-renting-a-home.pdf) supports rental deposits and lease-dependent costs. Sources are US-based; draft intentionally omits US tax deductions and jurisdiction-specific terms. The graphic states that contracts and local rules differ. It offers no personalized recommendation. Scene is a fictional city, not Riyadh.
