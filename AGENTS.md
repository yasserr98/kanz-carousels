Read `START-HERE.md`, `docs/agent-memory/INDEX.md`, and the selected library's unchanged `HOW-TO.md` before production. Use Codex for this workflow; Claude is not required.

- Work from supplied, separately approved copy. Do not create, recover or infer private writing prompts or Arabic voice formulas from examples.

- Keep genuine website screenshots, book covers and source attribution. No decorative slide/page counters.
- Duplicate the complete library into `work/<English Title>/` before editing; keep relative asset paths intact.

- Keep credentials, .env files, local account caches and customer data out of commits. Do not publish or grant access without authorization.
- Preserve unrelated edits. Record material production decisions and review status in `docs/agent-memory/INDEX.md`.
